Design and execution of experimental protocols under controlled and greenhouse conditions to study plant-pathogen interactions and soil microorganisms.

**Skills:** bacterial isolation and subculturing, plate streaking techniques, preparation and quality control of specialized culture media, and large-scale nucleic acid extraction (plant and bacterial DNA).

**Location:** Center for Cell and Molecular Biology Research (CIBCM-UCR) and Crop Protection Research Center (CIPROC-UCR).

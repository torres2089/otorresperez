Agricultural Engineer graduated from the University of Costa Rica. My profile combines technical management of production systems, crop protection, and the application of molecular and bioinformatics tools for diagnosing and analyzing soil health.

  <br>**Period:** September 2025 - Present

  <br>**Period:** March 2023 - July 2024 / January 2025 - July 2025

  <br><strong>Period:</strong> January 2025 - December 2025

## Technical Skills
* **Crop and Field Management:** Agricultural logistics and management of musaceous crops (banana family).
* **Crop Protection:** Phytosanitary diagnostics, soil sampling, and the use of cover crops (such as *Crotalaria*) for nematode and pathogen management.
* **Bioinformatics and Data:** Microbiome analysis through bioinformatics pipelines (QIIME 2, DADA2), high-performance computing clusters with Singularity, and scientific design tools (BioRender and Canva).

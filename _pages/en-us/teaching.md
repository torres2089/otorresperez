  * Evaluation of postharvest parameters, quality attributes, and experimental data collection in tropical export fruits (banana and pitahaya).
* **National Banana Corporation (CORBANA)** | *Research Internship*
  * Evaluation of agronomic descriptors for genetic improvement of musaceous crops (banana family).

  * Technical analysis of project outcomes, development of materials for strategic outreach, and administrative support tasks.

* **Center for Cell and Molecular Biology Research (CIBCM - UCR)** | *Laboratory and Greenhouse Assistant*
